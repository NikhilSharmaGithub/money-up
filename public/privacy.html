🎲 MoneyMove — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

MoneyMove is a multiplayer property-trading board game. We built it to be played, not to harvest data. This page explains exactly what the game stores and why.

What we store

  • A random device identity. When you first open the game, your device generates a random token. It is not your name, email, or phone number, and it cannot identify you outside the game. Your seat at a table, your friend code, your coins and your purchased cosmetics are all keyed to this token.
  • Your chosen nickname and country flag, so other players can see who they are playing with.
  • Game data. Rooms, moves, results and the in-game chat needed to run a live match. Finished-game summaries (map, winner, turn count) are kept for statistics.
  • Friend connections and direct messages. If you add a friend by code and chat with them, those messages are stored on the server so both of you can read the thread.
  • Optional sign-in. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we store only the provider's anonymous user identifier and the display name it shares, to link your profile across reinstalls. Signing in is never required to play.
  • Coin purchases. If you buy a coin pack, the charge is handled entirely by the App Store. All we receive and keep is the transaction identifier from the signed receipt, which is what stops the same purchase being credited twice. We never see or store card, bank or billing details.

What stays on your device only

  • Your phone number, if you enter one in Settings — it is never sent to the server.
  • Your match history list, table-style choice, and sound preference.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with third parties.
  • No payment details on our side — coin packs are charged by the App Store, and coins only ever buy cosmetics.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, or any device data beyond what is listed above.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your device identity, so nothing on the server can be linked back to you. To have your profile (nickname, friend code, coins, messages) wiped from the server as well, contact us with your friend code and we will delete it.

Children

MoneyMove does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the only free-text the game stores are nicknames and chat messages between players who exchanged friend codes.
