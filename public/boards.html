All nineteen MoneyMove boards

Every game is played on one of these. Some are the classic forty tiles, some are short and vicious, and eleven of them are single countries with their own cities, prices and sets. The host picks one before the game starts — or picks Shuffle and gets a different board every time.

The country boards

Eleven boards, each a tour of one country — real cities, ordered roughly by what they cost, so the cheap end of the board is somewhere different in every one. Play India and the expensive corner is Marine Drive; play Japan and it is Shibuya.

Shuffle

A freshly shuffled board every single game. Nobody at the table has played it before, which is the point — no memorised prices, no knowing which corner is the dangerous one.

Picking one

The host chooses the board in the lobby, under Board. If you want a game that ends inside half an hour, take Blitz. If you want the longest possible evening, take Mr. Worldwide. If you want the game everybody already knows the rules to, take Classic. The rules are the same on all of them.
