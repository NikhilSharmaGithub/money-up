🎲 MoneyMove — Support

How do I play with friends?

Create a game and share the room code (or the invite link on web). New rooms are private until you switch Private room off in the game settings, which lists them for everyone. Friends can also add each other with 6-character friend codes to chat and hop into each other's rooms.

My game disconnected — did I lose my seat?

No. A bot holds your seat for a bit, and the home screen offers Continue game to jump back in. Pass & play seats on the same device come back too.

How do coins work?

Collect a few coins every day from the daily reward, and a couple more for every game you win. Spend them in the Store on token skins, avatars and extra boards, or top up with an optional coin pack in the iPhone app. Nothing you can buy changes the odds: coins never make you better at the game.

Community rules

MoneyMove has zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats or spam — in table chat, messages, friend requests or player names. Players who break these rules are removed.

Someone is bothering me — how do I report or block them?

In the app, long-press their message in chat, or open the … menu in a message thread or on a friend or friend request, then choose Report or Block. Blocking works immediately: they drop off your friends, cannot message you or send you requests, and their chat is hidden from you. Every report reaches us and is reviewed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Account & help → Delete account. Your profile, coins, purchases, friends and messages are deleted permanently. You can also email us your friend code and we will do it for you.
